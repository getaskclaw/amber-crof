## Cross-model findings

1. **Four cases failed every model.** A-d511f9e8 produced the *identical* 4/12 on all five; A-cdc3d11a (adversarial review) went negative for everyone; A-ea80d793 (visual review) failed even the four vision-capable models; A-be92627f likewise universal. These mark the current frontier of the suite — and two are flagged for case-design review.
2. **qwen3.8-27b is the week's value king.** 14/21 — ties our frontier anchor — at $0.09/$0.30 pricing, including the first-ever full score (15/15) on A-a317e74b, a case no model had previously maxed.
3. **Same model name ≠ same capability across vendors.** glm-5.3-flash on crof scored 1/7 on A-442d4aab; the same-named model on Ollama Cloud scored 7/7 — **same day, same effort band, full library** ([amber-ollama W36](https://github.com/getaskclaw/amber-ollama/blob/main/results/2026-W36.md): ollama serve totals 15/21 vs crof's 13/21). Model names are vendor-endpoint-specific; benchmark accordingly.
4. **The vision beta fixes the worst behavior, not the score.** d4fv eliminated the base model's review-face fabrication (A-cdc3d11a: -12 → 0) and gained one build case, but still failed the vision case — vision capability ≠ visual-review competence.
5. **Small-model discipline gap.** qwen3.5-9b (9B) passed the suite's hardest build case (A-442d4aab 7/7) yet twice burned the entire time budget on one ops case without delivering (`∅` — over-long reasoning, not a quality score).
6. **deepseek text-only quirk:** d4f-0731 emits raw tool-call markup into its text stream when tools are disabled (verified 2026-09-06); harmless with tools on.
7. **The UI-build case is passable on crof after all.** glm-5.3-flash is the first crof model to pass A-d9b79b46 (initially mis-recorded as a failure — see Errata).

## Errata (2026-09-06, same day)

The first publication of this issue contained two errors, both ours (harness ops), both corrected above:

- **A-d9b79b46 (ui-build):** the judging host had a broken dependency (playwright) during the first pass, so three models' papers (d4fv, g53f, q35) could not be scored and were mis-recorded as model failures. After fixing the judge and re-scoring: g53f actually **passes**; d4fv and q35 remain real failures. g53f's total is therefore 13/21, not 12/21.
- **Frontier anchor figure:** we first cited 14/21, then corrected to 15/21 after re-reading its scoreboard, and now settle at **14/21** after a wire audit: the anchor's 2026-09-05 vision-case pass came from a session that had fallen back to a substitute model for one call, so it doesn't count. Clean-wire reruns of that case: fail (2.0, verdict FIX), fail (-2), pass (3.0) — genuinely borderline for the anchor; we count it as a fail for the official tally. Its ui-build and req-drift passes were re-confirmed on clean wires. Net: q38's 14/21 **ties** the anchor. (Lesson baked into our harness: any session containing a substitute-model call is discarded wholesale, and bench profiles now run with no fallback chain at all.)

Process fixes shipped the same day: the harness now classifies judge-side crashes and billing exhaustion as `invalid_infrastructure` (auto-retried) instead of model failures.
